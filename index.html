What the studio does

Four disciplines,

The studio

Independent
by design

LITrenders started with architectural visualization — interiors, exteriors, and the kind of images that sell a project before a single wall exists. The craft stayed; the medium moved.

Today most of the studio's time goes into Android apps, built end to end: the idea, the code, the art, the store listing, and the documentation you're reading now.
