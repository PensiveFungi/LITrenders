Website
privacy notice

Last updated September 9, 2026  ·  Covers litrenders.com. Each app has its own policy, linked from its page.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no trackers and loads nothing from a third-party CDN — the fonts and images are served from this domain. There is no form to fill in and no account to create.

Hosting

litrenders.com is a set of static files hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers process the technical information a browser sends in order to deliver a page — your IP address, the page requested, your user agent, and the time of the request. That processing is GitHub's, under GitHub's privacy statement and its GitHub Pages documentation. LITrenders has no access to those logs and receives no visitor data from them.

Email

If you email litrenders@gmail.com, that message and your address sit in an email account so it can be answered, and are kept only as long as is useful for that. They are not added to a mailing list, and they are not passed to anyone.

Links out

Some pages link to Google Play, GitHub, and the policies of services the apps use. Once you follow a link, that site's own privacy policy applies.

The apps

An app's privacy policy is specific to that app: Torchwake, IonDrive, Budget Calendar.
