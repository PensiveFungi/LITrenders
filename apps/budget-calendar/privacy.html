The short version

  • Your budget stays on your phone. Every entry, amount, note and category is stored in the app's own storage on your device.
  • There is no account, no sync server and no backup to LITrenders. We cannot see your numbers, because we never receive them.
  • The only network request the app makes is for currency exchange rates, from [EXCHANGE-RATE PROVIDER]. That request contains no budget data — it asks for rates, not for anything about you.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics, no tracking.

Who is behind the app

LITrenders is the trading name of Bruno Camargo, an independent developer. There is no company behind it and no team — one person builds these apps, and the same person reads the mail sent to litrenders@gmail.com.

Whenever this policy says “we”, it means exactly that one person.

What the app stores, and where

Budget Calendar keeps the following on your device only: your entries (dates, amounts, descriptions, categories), your currency and language choices, and your display settings. None of it is transmitted anywhere.

The one exception: exchange rates

To convert between currencies with up-to-date figures, the app periodically requests published rates from [EXCHANGE-RATE PROVIDER]. That request asks “what is the current rate for these currencies?” — it does not include your entries, your totals or an identifier for you.

As with any internet request, the provider's servers can see the network address (IP) the request came from and the currencies asked about. That is unavoidable for any app that fetches live rates, and it is handled under that provider's own privacy policy: [EXCHANGE-RATE PROVIDER PRIVACY POLICY URL]. If you never change currency, or use the app offline, that request is not made.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no cloud sync.
  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No bank connection. The app never asks for, and cannot access, your bank, card or account details.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising ID, no ads, no analytics SDK.
  • No selling or sharing of your financial information — it is never received in the first place.

Permissions the app asks for

  • INTERNET — used only to fetch currency exchange rates.

The app does not request storage, location, camera, microphone, contacts or account permissions.

Your data and how to remove it

  • Delete an entry: delete it in the app.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app, or use Android's Settings → Apps → Budget Calendar → Storage → Clear data.
  • Backups: if you have Android's own backup service enabled for the app, a copy may be held in your Google account under Google's terms, not ours. You control that in your device settings.

Because nothing is held on our side, a deletion request has nothing to act on — but if you want that confirmed in writing, ask at litrenders@gmail.com.

Children

Budget Calendar is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It is a personal finance tool intended for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, write to litrenders@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Security

No app can promise perfect security, and this one does not. What it can do is keep the amount of data worth attacking as close to zero as possible, which is the approach taken here: data that never leaves your device cannot be leaked from a server, because there is no server holding it.

Traffic to any third-party service listed above is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).

Changes to this policy

If Budget Calendar starts collecting or sharing something it does not collect today, this page is updated before that version reaches Google Play, and the “last updated” date at the top changes with it. Material changes are also noted in the release notes of the version that introduces them.

The current version of this policy always lives at this URL, which is the address given in the app's Google Play listing.
