The short version

  • IonDrive collects nothing. No account, no sign-in, no server.
  • Your times, settings and progress are saved on your device and nowhere else.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, no tracking of any kind.
  • The game runs fully offline.

Who is behind the game

LITrenders is the trading name of Bruno Camargo, an independent developer. There is no company behind it and no team — one person builds these apps, and the same person reads the mail sent to litrenders@gmail.com.

Whenever this policy says “we”, it means exactly that one person.

What the game stores

IonDrive saves lap times, control-scheme choices, calibration values and graphics settings in the app's own storage on your device. That data never leaves the phone: there is no LITrenders server for it to go to, and the game does not upload it anywhere.

There is no online leaderboard, no multiplayer, no profile and no login.

What the game does not do

  • No personal information — no name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • No location data.
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs.
  • No ads and no ad networks.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
  • No third-party SDK that collects data.

Permissions the game asks for

IonDrive requests no sensitive Android permissions. It does not need internet access to play; it does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone or contacts.

The game reads the device's motion sensors when you choose the tilt control scheme. Android does not treat motion sensors as a permission-gated feature, and the readings are used for steering in the moment and never stored or transmitted.

Removing your data

Uninstalling IonDrive removes everything it saved. Android's Settings → Apps → IonDrive → Storage → Clear data resets it without uninstalling. There is nothing held anywhere else, so there is nothing else to request the deletion of.

Children

IonDrive is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. It collects no data from anyone, of any age. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, write to litrenders@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Security

No app can promise perfect security, and this one does not. What it can do is keep the amount of data worth attacking as close to zero as possible, which is the approach taken here: data that never leaves your device cannot be leaked from a server, because there is no server holding it.

Traffic to any third-party service listed above is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).

Changes to this policy

If IonDrive starts collecting or sharing something it does not collect today, this page is updated before that version reaches Google Play, and the “last updated” date at the top changes with it. Material changes are also noted in the release notes of the version that introduces them.

The current version of this policy always lives at this URL, which is the address given in the app's Google Play listing.
