IonDrive

IonDrive is a hover racer built in Godot: flowing circuits, boost pads, and handling tuned for touchscreens rather than ported to them.

In development Support

What it is

Anti-gravity racing, built for a phone you already own.

  • Four control schemes, all first-class
    Touch zones, virtual stick, tilt with calibration, and gamepad. Pick one in the menu instead of being handed a default.
  • Hover physics with real weight
    Ships ride the track surface rather than sitting on it, with wide circuits and Wipeout-style boost pads.
  • Free flight
    An arcade 6DOF mode with soft bounds and no crashing, for learning a ship's handling without a timer.
  • A look, not a filter
    Stylized low-poly geometry, flat faceted shading and a dusk gradient palette, rendered crisp at full resolution.
  • Runs offline
    No account, no connection, no interruptions.
  • PlatformAndroid — Godot 4, Jolt physics
  • StatusIn development — not yet on Google Play
  • DeveloperLITrenders (Bruno Camargo)
