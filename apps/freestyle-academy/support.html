Get in touch

One address, for everything: litrenders@gmail.com. There is no ticket system, no bot and no support tier — the person who reads it is the person who wrote the app. Answers usually take a day or two. Write in English or Spanish, whichever you prefer.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. As a guest you can train in every mode and play Gallos and Master Serie battles. A guest keeps nothing, though: no streak, sessions, logros or rankings, and clearing the app's data loses it all. A free account keeps your streak, sessions and the free logros.

What does Plus add?

Plus is the one paid plan, a monthly subscription through Google Play. It removes the ads, and it is what earns XP: your recorded sessions are transcribed and scored by AI, and that score is your XP. It also unlocks Studio (songs and instrumentals made from your sessions, on daily credits), 100+ beats, the extra avatars, and a place in the Global ranking and the Ligas. The price is shown in Google Play in your currency.

How do I cancel Plus?

In Google Play: Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or from Planes in the app. You keep Plus until the end of the month already paid. Refunds follow Google Play's policies.

Why am I not in the rankings?

Global and Ligas rank XP, and only Plus earns XP, so only Plus accounts appear there. A free account can still read the Global table, open other MCs' profiles and count towards its crew in Tu Crew.

I got signed out on my other phone.

An account runs on one device at a time. Signing in on a new device ends the session on the previous one; your data stays in the account.

My recordings show as "No disponible en este dispositivo".

Recording and Studio audio stays on the device that made it; only its details (date, title, transcript) follow your account. On another device they appear greyed out instead of disappearing.

A Studio song says "Caduca en N días".

The generator deletes its files after 14 days, so the app saves each song to your phone as soon as it is ready. One marked with a countdown has not been saved yet, usually because it was made offline. Tap save while it is still available.

Do I have to give it microphone access?

No. Decline it and every round still runs. You lose the level meter and the recording, and with it the AI evaluation that awards XP.

My scores from the AI seem wrong.

They may well be. The AI transcribes your turn first and scores the transcript, so background noise, a distant microphone, heavy slang or very fast delivery all carry into the score. Treat it as a rough signal over many sessions. If an evaluation is out of line, report it from the results screen.

Reporting a bug

The quickest way is in the app: Ajustes → Ayuda → "Reportar un error". It attaches the device details (app and Android version, model, screen) for you, and says exactly what it sends before sending. Or write to litrenders@gmail.com with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead.

Feature requests

Welcome, and genuinely read. The app is built by one person, so most requests join a list rather than a schedule. Requests that describe a training problem — “I cannot practise X because…” — are more useful than requests for a specific button.

Deleting your data

  • With an account: Ajustes → Datos → "Borrar cuenta" deletes the account and all its data, on the device and in the cloud, and cancels the renewal of a Plus subscription.
  • Without the app: ask at https://academy-79fd1940.web.app/eliminar-cuenta, or write to litrenders@gmail.com from the address on the account.
  • As a guest: Ajustes → Datos → "Borrar datos" clears what is saved on the device; a guest has no account in the cloud.
