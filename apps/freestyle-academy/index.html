Freestyle Academy

A training room for improvised rap, built the way a practice session actually runs: pick a mode, set a round length, and the timer starts feeding you words to rhyme against. It runs right here in your browser — nothing to install, no account required, and your progress stays on your own machine. The app's interface is in Spanish.

What you are using

The real thing, not a mockup or a video. Pick a mode, choose how long a round should last, and train. The first session works without granting anything — the microphone is only asked for when you want the level meter and the round recording.

  • Your progress stays here. XP, streaks, achievements, your rhyme library and your session history are saved in this browser's own storage.
  • Nothing to install. No account and no sign-up to train. Signing in is a separate, optional choice that syncs your progress between devices.
  • The microphone is optional. Decline it and every mode still runs; you lose the level meter and the recording that the optional AI analysis needs.
  • Better full screen. The layout is phone-first, so the round timer and the mic meter have more room in their own tab than in this frame.

Clearing your browser data clears your progress with it, unless you have signed in and let it sync.

What it is

Reps, not theory.

  • Eleven training modes
    Free rounds, forced rhyme endings, stimulus words, images, themes and an incremental mode that shortens the clock under you — 60 seconds, then 45, then 30, then 20.
  • A round timer that behaves
    The countdown runs before the clock starts, so the seconds you spend getting ready are never charged to the round.
  • A rhyme library you can edit
    Grouped by ending, searchable mid-session, and yours to add to. Your additions survive updates to the built-in list.
  • Progress that accumulates
    XP per minute and per round, levels that cost more as you climb, practice streaks, and eight achievements with fixed thresholds.
  • Optional AI analysis
    When it is switched on and you are signed in, a round can be transcribed and scored on the five dimensions freestyle is usually judged by. It is off by default and never required.
  • Runs onAny modern browser, phone or desktop
  • LanguageSpanish interface
  • StorageYour own device — sign-in and sync are optional
  • MicrophoneOptional — asked for only when a session starts
  • DeveloperLITrenders (Bruno Camargo)

How it is built

A port, not a rewrite.

Freestyle Academy started as an Android app in Kotlin. This is the same product on the web: plain ES modules, no framework, no bundler and no build step, so what is served is what was written.

  • The scoring rules are the same numbers
    XP, levelling cost, streak behaviour, timer contract, stimulus rotation and the achievement thresholds were ported line for line from the Kotlin and checked against the same test values.
  • Local first, sync second
    Everything is written to the browser's storage under the same key names the Android app uses in its own preferences. Clearing site data is the equivalent of clearing app storage.
  • The API key never reaches the browser
    AI analysis goes through a small server-side function that holds the key and returns only the result. The browser never sees a credential.
  • Honest about what is missing
    Battles are not implemented and the app says so rather than pretending. Nothing in the interface promises a feature that has not been built.
  • A palette of its own
    The product is not the brand. Freestyle Academy keeps its own dark, saturated look inside the frame while the page around it stays in the studio's ink and paper.
