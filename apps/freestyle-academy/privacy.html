This is an English translation of the Política de Privacidad shown in the app (version 9). If the two differ, the Spanish text governs.

This policy explains what data Freestyle Academy handles, why, who it is shared with and how you can control it. The data controller is Bruno Camargo (litrenders@gmail.com).

1. What data we handle

  • Account: your email address and the identifier Firebase assigns to your account. If you sign in with Google, the email and name Google gives us. We do not store your password: Firebase Authentication handles it.
  • Profile: your AKA, crew, avatar and punchline.
  • Progress: XP, level, league, points, streak (racha), number of sessions, practice time and session history.
  • Recordings and transcripts: the audio of your sessions and the transcribed text of each turn.
  • The rhymes and library history you create.
  • Other participants: in a battle on your device, each participant's voice during their turn and the name you type for them. The most recent names are remembered on your device only, so you can pick them again; if you have an account, the name of whoever rapped each turn is also saved with your list of recordings.
  • Subscription: if you take Plus, the purchase identifier Google Play gives us, the plan, its status and its end date. Google processes the payment: we never see or store your payment details.
  • Studio usage: the credits you spend each day and the time of each generation, to apply the daily limit.
  • Bug reports: what you write, plus the device's technical context (app version, Android version, model, processor, language, screen size, free space, plan and whether you are signed in).
  • A random installation identifier, which does not identify your hardware, used for the one-device rule and to limit abuse of the bug report form and of beat previews.
  • The advertising identifier and ad usage data handled by Google AdMob, and your ad consent choice.
  • Reports and blocks: the reports you send (reason, details, the reported content and a copy of the reported profile as it was) and the list of MCs you have blocked.
  • Age: only whether you meet the minimum age; we do not store your date of birth.
  • Diagnostics and usage: if the app crashes, a technical crash report (model, Android and app version, and where in the code), and a small set of usage events (sign-up, completed sessions, visits to Planes, Studio generations), tied to an installation identifier and, if you have an account, to its identifier. They do not include your recordings, your text or your advertising identifier.

2. What we use it for

To provide the service: creating and maintaining your account, saving your progress, syncing it between sessions, evaluating your sessions with AI and awarding XP, showing the rankings, generating songs in Studio and handling the bugs you report.

To keep the service secure: applying the one-device-per-account rule and Studio's daily credits, preventing abuse of the forms, and reviewing content reports.

To support the free plan: showing ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before showing ads that use your data; you can change your choice in Ajustes → Legal → "Privacidad de anuncios".

To improve the app: finding and fixing crashes and understanding which parts are used. If you turn on the streak reminder in Ajustes, the app sends you a local notification; you can turn it off at any time.

The legal basis is performing the contract for using the app, your consent where the law requires it (for example for personalised advertising) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

3. The microphone and your recordings

The app only uses the microphone when a turn starts and you have granted the permission. The recording is saved on your device, and when the session ends you decide whether to keep it or discard it.

The audio of your recordings and of your Studio songs and instrumentals stays on the device where you made them: we do not store it in the cloud. If you have an account, we do keep their list (date, mode, title, participants and transcript), so that on another device they show up as unavailable instead of disappearing.

To evaluate a session and award XP, the audio is sent to Groq, which transcribes and scores it. If you use Studio to generate a song from a recording, that recording is sent to Kie (Suno) to generate it. Neither happens unless you start it, and if you discard the audio we keep the session's transcript for your own history.

4. Who it is shared with

We work with providers that handle data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database and functions): account, profile, progress, history, and the list of your recordings and Studio songs with their transcripts.
  • Google AdMob: advertising.
  • Google Play: subscription payments, and verifying each purchase.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics and Remote Config: crash reports, usage events and the app's remote configuration.
  • Groq: the audio of the sessions that are evaluated, and its transcript.
  • Kie (Suno): the recordings you send to generate a song.

These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, with the safeguards the applicable rules provide for. We do not sell your data.

5. What is public

If you have an account, your ranking row is visible to other registered users: AKA, crew, avatar, punchline, plan, league, points, streak, sessions, practice time and recent activity. Nothing else in your account is accessible to other users: your recordings, transcripts and email are not.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as you keep your account. When you delete it from Ajustes, the account and all its data are removed immediately from the device and from the cloud.

The Studio usage record (credits spent each day) is also deleted automatically within two days at most, even if you keep your account.

Bug reports and content reports are kept as long as needed to look into them and act. If you delete your account, they are no longer linked to it.

7. Your rights

You can access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and ask for it to be ported.

In the app: Perfil → Editar perfil corrects your profile data, and Ajustes → Datos → "Borrar cuenta" deletes your account and all its data from the device and the cloud. Without the app, you can ask us to delete your account at https://academy-79fd1940.web.app/eliminar-cuenta.

To exercise any other right, write to litrenders@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country (in Uruguay, the Unidad Reguladora y de Control de Datos Personales).

8. Minors

The app is not aimed at anyone under the minimum age set in the terms (16). If we find data from someone below that age, we delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update it. When a change is significant we ask you to accept it again inside the app, and you can always read it from Ajustes → Legal. For any question: litrenders@gmail.com.
