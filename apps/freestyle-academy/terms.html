These terms govern your use of the Freestyle Academy app (the "App"), operated by Bruno Camargo. By creating an account, entering as a guest or using the App you accept these terms. If you do not agree with them, do not use the App.

1. Who provides the service

The App is offered by Bruno Camargo. You can write to us at any time at litrenders@gmail.com.

2. Minimum age

You must be at least 16 to use the App. When you enter we ask for your date of birth: we do not store it, only whether you meet the age. If you are a minor, you also need the permission of your parents or legal guardians. If we find that an account belongs to someone who does not meet this requirement, we may close it.

3. Account and guest

You can use the App as a guest or with an account. As a guest, your activity is saved only on that device and earns no XP, level or account achievements; if you uninstall the App or clear its data, it is lost.

With an account, you are responsible for your credentials and for the activity carried out from it. An account is used on one device at a time: signing in on a new device automatically ends the session open on the previous one.

4. Your content

The recordings, lyrics, songs and texts you create in the App are yours and remain yours. So that we can provide the service, you grant us a limited, non-exclusive, revocable licence to store, transcribe, process and show you that content, and to send it to the providers that make transcription, AI evaluation and song generation possible, as the Privacy policy details.

This licence exists only to run the App. We do not use your recordings for advertising and we do not publish them: whatever is shared outside the App is shared by you, when you choose.

In a battle on your device, each participant's turn is recorded, with the name you type for them, and that recording can be sent for evaluation like any other. Before recording other people you must have their permission to record them and to process their voice and name as the Privacy policy describes. You are responsible for getting it.

5. Rules of use

Freestyle welcomes rivalry and provocation, but not everything goes. By using the App you agree not to:

  • Post content that incites hatred or violence, or harass, threaten or discriminate against anyone because of their origin, ethnicity, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or disability.
  • Upload content that is illegal, sexual content involving minors, or content that infringes the rights of others (including copyright in beats or instrumentals).
  • Impersonate another person, or choose an AKA, crew or punchline that impersonates, insults or identifies someone else.
  • Manipulate the rankings, automate use of the App, or try to access other accounts' data.

We may remove content, reset an AKA or suspend an account that breaks these rules.

You can report another MC's profile, and any AI-generated content (a session's evaluation or a Studio song), from within the App; we review every report. You can also block an MC: you will no longer see their AKA, crew, avatar and punchline, and they are not told.

6. Public profile and rankings

If you have an account and take part in the Freestyle League, your ranking row is visible to other registered users. It includes your AKA, crew, avatar, punchline, plan, league, points, streak, number of sessions, practice time and your activity over the last few days.

Your recordings, transcripts, email and the rest of your account data are not public, and no other user can read them.

7. Plans, credits and advertising

The App offers a free plan with advertising and a paid plan, Plus, which requires an account. It is a monthly subscription bought and billed through Google Play, and Google Play's terms also apply to it. The price, in your currency, and any introductory offer are shown in Google Play before you confirm the purchase.

The subscription renews automatically every month until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time from your Google Play account (also from Planes in the App); you keep the plan until the end of the period already paid and the next renewal is not charged. Refunds follow Google Play's policies.

By confirming the purchase you ask for the plan to start immediately. If the law of your country gives you a right of withdrawal for digital content or services, you agree that you lose it once the plan is active, to the extent that law allows.

If you delete your account, we cancel your subscription's renewal.

Battles on your device (Gallos and Master Serie) cost nothing. On the free plan, and without an account, an ad is shown each time you enter one.

Studio runs on daily credits: each day you have an amount for creating songs, instrumentals and albums, and what you do not use that day does not carry over. A generation that fails does not use them. The current amounts are shown in the App and may change. Credits are an internal feature: they are not money, have no value outside the App, and are neither transferable nor refundable.

8. Availability of the service

We work to keep the App running, but it is provided "as is" and may suffer interruptions, errors or changes. Some features need an internet connection and third-party services: without them, that part of the App does not work (for example, without a connection a session is not evaluated and earns no XP). We may add, change or remove features.

9. Limitation of liability

To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect damage or for lost content, and in particular we are not liable for losing recordings that exist only on your device. Nothing in these terms limits the rights the law gives you as a consumer.

10. Leaving and deletion

You can stop using the App and delete your account at any time from Ajustes → Datos → "Borrar cuenta". The account and all its data (profile, progress, rhymes, history, recordings and Studio songs) are removed from the device and from the cloud, and your row disappears from the rankings. If you no longer have the App, you can ask for it at https://academy-79fd1940.web.app/eliminar-cuenta or by writing to litrenders@gmail.com.

As a guest, "Borrar datos" deletes the activity saved on that device. We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. When a change is significant we will ask you to accept them again inside the App before continuing, and you can always read them from Ajustes → Legal.
