The AI scoring, and what it is worth

The optional analysis transcribes a round and scores it. Both steps are done by a machine learning model, and both are fallible:

  • The transcript can be wrong, and is more likely to be wrong with background noise, a distant microphone, slang or fast delivery.
  • The scores are generated from that transcript, so an inaccurate transcript produces inaccurate scores. They are a practice aid and a rough signal over time. They are not a judgement of your ability, not a ranking against anyone else, and carry no weight outside this app.
  • Written feedback produced by the model may be generic, mistaken, or unhelpful for your particular round.

Do not treat the output as authoritative, and do not rely on it for anything that matters. It is offered as it is, with no promise of accuracy.
