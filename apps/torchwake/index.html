Torchwake

Torchwake runs a full tabletop-style campaign for one player. Dice, checks, combat and your AI party members are deterministic code — the language model only narrates what the rules already decided.

In development Support

What it is

Solo tabletop roleplaying with an AI Game Master.

  • A Game Master that narrates, not decides
    Every roll, check and outcome is resolved by a deterministic rules engine. The AI narrator reads the result and turns it into a scene, so the game can never be argued into a different answer.
  • A party that plays itself
    Companion characters are programmed behaviour, not chat bots. They act on the same rules you do.
  • Character creator with generated portraits
    Build the sheet, then get an AI-generated portrait to go with it.
  • Three voice modes
    Silent, free on-device Android text-to-speech, or a metered cloud narrator voice on paid tiers.
  • The Record
    A complete linear log of every action, roll and message from the session, kept as a secondary view you can scroll back through.
  • PlatformAndroid — Kotlin & Jetpack Compose
  • StatusIn development — not yet on Google Play
  • DeveloperLITrenders (Bruno Camargo)
