The short version

  • Torchwake has no account, no login and no LITrenders server. Your characters and campaigns are stored on your device.
  • When AI narration is switched on, the text describing what just happened in your game is sent to a third-party AI provider ([AI PROVIDER]) so it can be written into a scene. That text is what your game generated: character names you chose, actions you took, and the results the rules engine produced.
  • The cloud narrator voice (paid tiers only) sends the narration text to a speech provider ([TTS PROVIDER]) to be spoken.
  • Character portraits are generated by an image provider ([IMAGE PROVIDER]) from the description you enter.
  • There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, and no analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Who is behind the app

LITrenders is the trading name of Bruno Camargo, an independent developer. There is no company behind it and no team — one person builds these apps, and the same person reads the mail sent to litrenders@gmail.com.

Whenever this policy says “we”, it means exactly that one person.

What the app sends, and when

Torchwake is built so that the game itself — dice, checks, combat, party behaviour, progression — runs entirely on your device as ordinary deterministic code. Nothing needs to be sent anywhere for the game to work. Three optional features do involve a network request:

1. AI narration

When narration is enabled, the app sends a short description of the current game state to [AI PROVIDER]: the names and attributes of the characters in the scene, the action taken, and the outcome the rules engine already decided. It receives narration text back. This text is generated from data you created inside the game. It is not linked to your name, your email, your device identifier or an account, because the app has none of those.

2. Cloud narrator voice (paid tiers)

If you choose the cloud voice instead of the free on-device Android text-to-speech, the narration text is sent to [TTS PROVIDER] and returned as audio. On-device text-to-speech sends nothing off the device.

3. Character portraits

When you generate a portrait in the character creator, the description you wrote is sent to [IMAGE PROVIDER] and an image comes back. The image is stored on your device.

All three features can be turned off. With narration set to text-only and portraits left ungenerated, Torchwake makes no network requests at all beyond what Google Play itself needs for a purchase.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no profile.
  • No name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • No location data of any kind.
  • No advertising ID, no ads, no ad networks.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting SDK.
  • No microphone or camera access.
  • No selling or renting of data to anyone, ever. There is nothing to sell.

Permissions the app asks for

  • INTERNET — required for AI narration, the cloud voice, portrait generation and Play Billing.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — required to offer subscriptions through Google Play.

Torchwake does not request storage, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

Your data and how to remove it

Everything Torchwake keeps — characters, campaigns, the session Record, generated portraits and your settings — is stored in the app's own storage on your device.

  • Delete one thing: delete the character or campaign inside the app.
  • Delete everything: uninstalling the app removes all of it. Android's Settings → Apps → Torchwake → Storage → Clear data does the same without uninstalling.
  • Subscriptions: managed in Google Play, not in the app. Cancelling is done from your Play account.
  • Requests: if you want confirmation that nothing of yours is held anywhere, write to litrenders@gmail.com. Because there is no account system, there is normally nothing to look up — but you will get an answer.

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or a similar law applies to you, the rights those laws grant — access, correction, deletion, portability, objection — are honoured. In practice the app holds nothing that could identify you, so most requests are answered by explaining exactly that.

Children

Torchwake is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The app's Google Play content rating is shown on its store listing. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, write to litrenders@gmail.com and it will be deleted.

Security

No app can promise perfect security, and this one does not. What it can do is keep the amount of data worth attacking as close to zero as possible, which is the approach taken here: data that never leaves your device cannot be leaked from a server, because there is no server holding it.

Traffic to any third-party service listed above is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).

Where data is processed

The providers listed above may process requests on servers outside your country, including in the United States. When you enable a feature that sends data to one of them, that transfer is what makes the feature work. Each provider's own policy governs where and how it processes the request.

Changes to this policy

If Torchwake starts collecting or sharing something it does not collect today, this page is updated before that version reaches Google Play, and the “last updated” date at the top changes with it. Material changes are also noted in the release notes of the version that introduces them.

The current version of this policy always lives at this URL, which is the address given in the app's Google Play listing.
