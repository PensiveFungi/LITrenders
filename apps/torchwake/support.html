Common questions

Do I need an internet connection to play?

Only for the optional AI features. With narration set to text-only and no portrait generation, Torchwake plays entirely offline.

Is the Game Master making up the rules as it goes?

No. Dice, checks, combat and progression are resolved by a deterministic rules engine written as ordinary code. The AI reads the result and narrates it, so it cannot decide that you succeeded when the dice said otherwise.

Can I play with other people?

No. Torchwake is single-player by design — that decision is what keeps it simple, and there are no plans to add multiplayer.

What do the paid tiers actually give me?

The cloud narrator voice and generated imagery are metered services that cost money to run, so they sit behind a subscription. The game itself — rules, campaigns, party, the Record — does not depend on them.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In the Google Play app: Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Cancelling there stops the next renewal. Subscriptions cannot be cancelled from inside the app, because Google handles the billing.

The narrator stopped mid-session. What happened?

Most likely the connection dropped or a metered allowance ran out. The rules engine keeps running either way — switch narration to text-only and the session continues.

Reporting a bug

Bug reports are welcome and genuinely useful. The ones that get fixed fastest include:

  • Your device model and Android version.
  • The app version (shown in the app's settings or on its Play listing).
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if the problem is visible.
  • Whether it happens every time or only sometimes.

If the problem involves narration, note whether narration was set to text, on-device voice or cloud voice when it happened.

Feature requests

Send them. Torchwake is built by one person, so not everything can be done, and things that are done take the time they take — but requests genuinely shape what gets built next, and you will get an honest answer about whether yours is likely to happen.

Deleting your data

Torchwake has no account, so there is no server-side profile to delete. Everything it keeps is on your device.

  • Delete individual content — characters, campaigns and Records can be deleted inside the app.
  • Delete all app data — Android Settings → Apps → Torchwake → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall.
  • Subscription data — held by Google Play under your Google account, and managed there.
  • Written confirmation — email litrenders@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request” and you will get a reply confirming what is and is not held.
